Who we are

A consultancy built around the three complaints we heard most

Ask anyone in India who has hired a lawyer what went wrong and you tend to hear the same three things: the fee kept moving, nobody returned the call, and the documents were impossible to understand.

Those are not hard problems to solve. They persist because the profession has little incentive to solve them. We built our practice on the opposite assumption — that a client who understands their own matter is a better client, not a harder one.

Today we act for individuals, startups and established businesses across the country, from tribunal representation through to routine registrations. The scale has changed. The three rules have not.
